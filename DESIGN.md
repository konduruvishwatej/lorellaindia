## Brand & Style

This design system translates the timeless romance, botanical craftsmanship, and bespoke hospitality of an artisanal café into a refined digital experience. The brand voice is cultured, warm, poetic, and quietly luxurious. It speaks to connoisseurs of specialty coffee, artisanal patisserie, and slow living who value intentional craft over industrial hustle.

The aesthetic philosophy draws heavily from late 19th-century Art Nouveau and Victorian botanical engraving, mediated through contemporary high-fashion editorial minimalism. Rich negative space bathed in warm, unbleached parchment tones creates a calm, sensory atmosphere, while deliberate strokes of deep carmine red deliver dramatic, focused poise. Tactile elegance is achieved not through drop shadows or glossy surfaces, but through hairline borders, delicate filigree framing, generous typographic leading, and organic ornamentation inspired by hand-carved woodblocks and copperplate press prints.

## Colors

The palette is built around the authentic warmth of heirloom papers and aged crimson inks:

- **Primary (`#8C1D24` — Vintage Carmine Red):** The anchor for brand identity, primary actions, prominent serifs, and signature botanical line art. It commands attention without being harsh, exuding warmth and heritage.
- **Secondary (`#C59B63` — Burnished Gold Leaf):** Used sparingly for badges, tasting notes, delicate accents, and decorative flourishes to complement the crimson.
- **Tertiary (`#2B4C3F` — Pressed Botanical Green):** A muted, deep herbal tone reserved for origin tags, vegan/dietary callouts, and organic sourcing markers.
- **Neutral Background (`#FAF7F0` — Warm Alabaster Parchment):** The foundation canvas that replaces sterile digital white with a tactile, cream-linen warmth. Paired with secondary surface tiers: `#FFFDF9` (Pure Ivory for elevated cards and modals) and `#F0ECE1` (Muted Linen for subtle contrasts, rules, and container fills).
- **Text & Structure Neutrals:** Primary body copy uses `#2A2223` (Charcoal Umber) for softer contrast than pure black, ensuring high readability while preserving historical charm. Muted copy utilizes `#736865` (Warm Slate).

## Typography

Typography embodies the balance between literary romance and contemporary digital precision:

- **Display & Headlines (EB Garamond):** Brings grace, sculpted ligatures, and genuine historical proportion. It is applied to menu item titles, featured origin stories, section headlines, and grand hero intros. Italics in EB Garamond are embraced for tasting descriptors, sensory pull-quotes, and seasonal highlights.
- **Body Text (Newsreader):** Selected for extended reading comfort and rhythmic optical cadence. It preserves the editorial atmosphere of classic menu bookplates, culinary histories, and detailed origin notes.
- **Microcopy, Badges & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers an understated, legible sans-serif counterweight. Rendered with tracked-out spacing in uppercase (e.g., `SPECIALITY CAFÉ`, `ORIGIN: HUILA, COLOMBIA`) to evoke curated botanical placards and Parisian apothecary labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an airy, editorial fluid grid:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers respect an editorial max-width of 1200px to maintain comfortable reading lines and room for botanical side motifs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Stacking transitions are linear and spacious, preventing visual density from crowding out the relaxed café mood.

Whitespace is treated as an active luxury ingredient: menu cards, coffee origins, and product showcases must feature generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to feel like individual art plates rather than compressed data rows.

## Elevation & Depth

To remain faithful to classic lithography and heritage stationery, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows or synthetic gloss. Instead, depth is achieved through tactile flat layers and delicate graphic borders:

- **Base Surface:** Canvas starts on `#FAF7F0`.
- **Raised Surfaces & Cards:** Shift to `#FFFDF9`, defined strictly by a 1px hairline border in `#8C1D24` at 18% opacity or `#E6DFC8` for understated panels.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** Overlay panels feature a warm translucent backdrop blur (`rgba(250, 247, 240, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) bounded by a crisp 1px `#8C1D24` frame and a soft, tinted ambient tone (`0 16px 40px -12px rgba(140, 29, 36, 0.08)`).
- **Art Nouveau Cartouche Dividers:** Content sections are separated by horizontal rules that terminate in custom floral centerpieces or double hairlines with a 2px offset.

## Shapes

The geometry adheres to subtle, hand-trimmed bookbinding lines:
- **Corner Curvature:** Uses soft radius tokens (`roundedness: 1`). Standard containers, menu panels, and cards employ a delicate 4px (`0.25rem`) corner, avoiding hard brutalism while eschewing modern pill or bubble silhouettes.
- **Organic Arch & Cameo Frames:** Special image containers and featured origin spotlights introduce oval, arched, or cameo crops directly echoing the rounded Art Nouveau crest of the café logo.
- **Fine Borders:** Borders are kept strictly at 1px thickness to mimic fine pen-and-ink linework.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid background in Vintage Carmine (`#8C1D24`), text in Warm Parchment (`#FAF7F0`), styled in `label-caps` (Plus Jakarta Sans, uppercase, tracked `0.18em`). Soft 4px radius, padding `0.85rem 2rem`. Hover state introduces a smooth shift to deep maroon (`#6E141A`) with a subtle 1px inner hairline.
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined with a 1px border in `#8C1D24`, transparent background, text in `#8C1D24`. On hover, fills with a sheer crimson wash (`rgba(140, 29, 36, 0.06)`).
- **Text / Editorial Action:** EB Garamond italic link with an animated bottom hairline that expands from the center outward on hover, accompanied by a small botanical leaf or floral glyph.

### Cards & Menu Items
- **Tasting / Product Card:** Built on `#FFFDF9` surface with a 1px hairline perimeter of `#E8E0D0`. Top imagery uses an arched crop (cameo effect). Title rendered in `headline-sm` (EB Garamond), followed by flavor notes in `body-sm` italic Newsreader, anchored by an tracked-out uppercase price and origin label.
- **Menu Row Item:** Two-column ledger style. Item name on the left, dotted leader line in muted carmine spanning the gap, price on the right in EB Garamond bold.

### Chips & Origin Tags
- Rendered in a pill or soft-rounded format using a gentle cream tint (`#F0ECE1`), 1px outline in `#8C1D24` (20% opacity), and text styled in `label-sm` (`#8C1D24` or `#2B4C3F` for single-origin / farm-direct indicators).

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Grounded in minimal stationery design. Fields feature a 1px bottom border in `#8C1D24` (or a full hairline box with `#E2DAC7` border), `#FFFDF9` fill, with the field label floating gracefully in `label-caps`. Focus states transition the border smoothly to solid `#8C1D24` with zero ring glow.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with soft 2px corners (checkboxes) and circles (radios), outlined in 1px `#8C1D24`. Checked state fills solid `#8C1D24` with a delicate, ivory hand-drawn checkmark or central dot.

### Ornaments & Framed Vignettes
- Component suites incorporate responsive SVG botanical dividers, corner floral flourishes, and crest bookends directly derived from the central rose, acanthus vine, and scrolling foliage of the café monogram.
